# Mohua Das, PhD

**Cancer genomics · Single-cell transcriptomics · Drug discovery**

I study treatment resistance in cancer by combining transcriptomic analysis with chemical biology and experimental oncology. My work connects changes in tumor-cell states and gene expression to drug response, metastasis, and tumor–immune interactions.

I hold a PhD in Pharmacy from the National University of Singapore, with research training in medicinal chemistry and chemical biology. My research at the Genome Institute of Singapore, A*STAR, has focused on colorectal cancer, single-cell genomics, and therapeutic discovery.

[ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8891-6333) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dmohua/) · [Research code](https://github.com/MD-Chem/SERPINE1)

## Featured research: SERPINE1 / RESIST-M

**How does resistance to oxaliplatin relate to metastatic behavior in colorectal cancer?**

Our study identified a SERPINE1-associated gene signature, RESIST-M, and examined its relationship to patient outcomes and colorectal cancer subtypes. I am an equal-first author, with contributions to genomic data processing, patient-cohort analysis, and interpretation of the findings.

The [SERPINE1 repository](https://github.com/MD-Chem/SERPINE1) contains R scripts for single-cell analysis of HCT116 cells and patient-cohort analyses, including gene-signature comparisons, survival analysis, and expression heatmaps.

[Read the paper — Cell Death & Disease (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41419-025-07855-y) · [Explore the code and data requirements](https://github.com/MD-Chem/SERPINE1#readme)
